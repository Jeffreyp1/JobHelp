# Cover Letter — Industry

JobHelp's default cover letter format targets industry roles. Length is **250-300 words** in **3 paragraphs**: HOOK, EVIDENCE, CLOSING.

## P1 — HOOK (~60-90 words)

Connect the company's product, technology, paper, or recent move to one specific candidate achievement. State the candidate's identity and the position.

Requirements:

- Open with a specific reference to the company's actual work — product name, recent launch, paper, engineering blog post, public talk, strategic move. Generic openers ("I am writing to express my interest...") are forbidden — see `04-banned-phrases.md`.
- Minimize subfield jargon. The first reader is often an HR recruiter without domain background.
- State the position by exact title and the candidate's identity in one sentence.
- One concrete hook from research, not a paraphrase of the JD.

## P2 — EVIDENCE (~120-150 words)

Two or three achievements translated to business value. Mirror JD terms. Frame as deliverables.

Requirements:

- 2-3 achievements only. More than 3 reads as a fact sheet.
- Maximum 3-4 quantified claims across the paragraph.
- Use the JD's vocabulary verbatim where it fits the candidate's truthful work (see `07-reframing-strategies.md`).
- Translate research/technical work into business outcome.
- Bridges go here when needed, framed honestly (see `08-bridge-language.md`).

## P3 — CLOSING (~60-80 words)

Forward-looking value plus active call to action.

Requirements:

- One sentence on what the candidate would bring to this specific team or product.
- Active call to action — "I'd welcome a conversation about..." or "Available to discuss..." Not the passive "Thank you for your consideration."
- If pivoting domains, address "why industry" positively. No defensive "Despite my background..." (see `04-banned-phrases.md`).
- Include availability or next step if appropriate.

## Universal CL rules

- The CL deepens what the resume presents — never introduces claims not traceable to a resume bullet.
- Limit total quantified claims to 3-5.
- Credentials woven into body paragraphs, not dumped in closing.
- Active voice. First-person specificity ("I built", not "Was responsible for building").
- Use the company's actual name and the candidate's contact details exactly.

## Em-dash cap (mandatory)

Maximum **2 em-dashes** per cover letter, counting both `---` and `—` (see `05-structural-rules.md`). CLs accumulate em-dashes faster than any other document type.

## Industry CL anti-patterns

- Generic opener -> Tier 1 fix.
- Defensive framing ("Despite my background...") -> rewrite via methodology bridge.
- Credential dump in closing -> weave into P2.
- Repeating resume bullets verbatim -> CL adds narrative context, not paraphrase.
- Sentence-length monotony -> see `05-structural-rules.md`.
- Rhetorical questions -> banned, reads as AI.
- More than 5 quantified claims total -> trim.

## Anti-fabrication still applies

Every claim in the CL must trace to a resume bullet which traces to the source record. The CL is not a place to introduce new metrics. See `02-anti-fabrication.md`.

## Self-check before returning

- [ ] Word count is 250-300 words.
- [ ] 3 paragraphs: HOOK / EVIDENCE / CLOSING.
- [ ] P1 references something specific about the company.
- [ ] No banned words or phrases.
- [ ] Em-dash count <= 2.
- [ ] Every claim traces to a resume bullet.
- [ ] CL deepens the resume; does not duplicate.
