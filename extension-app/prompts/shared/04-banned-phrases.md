# Banned Phrases

Phrase-level AI tells. These compound across a document — even one banned phrase per page reads as generic.

## Opening / transition phrases

- "In today's rapidly evolving..."
- "At the forefront of..."
- "It is worth noting that..."
- "This experience has taught me..."
- "I am uniquely positioned to..."
- "In an era of..."
- "In a world where..."
- "As organizations increasingly..."

## Resume / CL specific

- "proven track record"
- "passionate about" — replace with a specific, evidenced interest
- "I am excited to apply" — replace with a concrete reason for applying
- "demonstrated ability to" — just state what you did
- "strong foundation in"
- "well-versed in"
- "adept at"
- "results-driven professional"
- "self-starter with a passion for"
- "dedicated to delivering excellence"
- "Responsible for" / "duties included" — replace with action verb + outcome (see 06-bullet-construction)

## Academic / research

- "groundbreaking research"
- "cutting-edge methodology"
- "novel approach" — say what is new about it
- "significant contributions to the field"
- "at the intersection of X and Y" — name the specific intersection (e.g., "applying Bayesian inference to single-cell RNA-seq")

## Cover letter generic openers (forbidden)

- "I am writing to express my interest in..."
- "I am writing to apply for..."
- "Please accept my application for..."
- "I came across your posting..."

A CL must open with a specific reference to the company's work — a product, paper, program, person, or recent move — not a generic statement of intent.

## Defensive / apologetic framing (forbidden)

- "Despite my background in..."
- "While I lack direct experience with..."
- "Although my degree is in..."

If pivoting domains, lead with the methodology bridge (see `08-bridge-language.md`), not an apology.

## Why these matter

Any one of these phrases pattern-matches to the most common AI-resume training data. Recruiters and HMs have read these literal sentences thousands of times. They signal "generic AI output," which is the exact opposite of the candidate's value.

## Replacement strategy

Cut. Then write the specific, concrete, named version. If the phrase was carrying the only content in the sentence, the sentence had no content — drop it entirely.

## Scan before returning

Before presenting any draft, search the document for each phrase above. Any hit is a Tier 1 fix — rewrite before the user sees it.
